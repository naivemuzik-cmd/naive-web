(Naive)

my history

NAIVE is the alias of Mauro Mart, a DJ and electronic music producer based in Buenos Aires, Argentina, with over 12 years of experience in the international scene. Known for blending Melodic House, Indie Dance and techno, NAIVE has released music on labels including Natura Viva, Balkan Connection, 3rd Avenue, Lump Records and Tropical Twista Records. His international career includes performances in London (Bloop), Spain (Garage 442, Macarena Club), Ibiza (Vibra District), Turkey (Bonjuk Bay), Austria, and a residency at Rio Electronic Music.
